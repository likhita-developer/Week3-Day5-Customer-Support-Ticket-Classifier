TASK 2 - PRIORITY ERROR ANALYSIS

The priority model made several mistakes because the ticket text contains
limited information about urgency. Many tickets describe the problem but
do not clearly indicate whether the issue is High, Medium, or Low priority.
The class imbalance also affects prediction performance.

10 Confusing Examples

1. Text:
please help me unable to sign in. please check and resolve it.
Actual: Medium
Predicted: Low
Reason:
The ticket describes a login problem but does not contain strong urgency
words, so the model predicted the majority Low class.

2. Text:
i need your help with need to update profile. i need this issue resolved.
Actual: Low
Predicted: Medium
Reason:
The phrase "need this issue resolved" may make the model interpret the
ticket as more urgent than it actually is.

3. Text:
could you please help problem with account. please help me as soon as possible.
Actual: Medium
Predicted: Low
Reason:
The ticket has an account-related problem, but the wording does not clearly
distinguish Medium from Low priority.

4. Text:
i am facing an issue where need help updating account. kindly check my account.
Actual: Medium
Predicted: Low
Reason:
The request is polite and general without strong urgency indicators.

5. Text:
could you please help password is not working. please help me as soon as possible.
Actual: Low
Predicted: Medium
Reason:
The phrase "as soon as possible" may cause the model to assign a higher
priority even though the actual label is Low.

6. Text:
i am unable to resolve this issue because page is not loading. please provide a solution.
Actual: Medium
Predicted: Low
Reason:
The ticket describes a technical problem but does not provide clear
priority-related words.

7. Text:
could you please help order is delayed. i have been facing this problem recently.
Actual: Low
Predicted: Medium
Reason:
The word "delayed" can suggest urgency, causing the model to predict
Medium instead of Low.

8. Text:
i need assistance because application is not working. this problem started today.
Actual: Medium
Predicted: Low
Reason:
The model recognizes the problem but the text does not contain enough
information to determine the priority correctly.

9. Text:
please check this problem payment was declined. can you look into this?
Actual: Medium
Predicted: Low
Reason:
The payment issue identifies the category clearly, but there is little
information about urgency or customer impact.

10. Text:
i need your help with order has not arrived. this problem started today.
Actual: Medium
Predicted: Low
Reason:
The ticket describes an order problem but does not explicitly indicate
high urgency, business impact, or time sensitivity.

Overall Observation

Category classification performs very well because ticket text contains
clear keywords related to each category. Priority classification is more
difficult because the dataset contains fewer High-priority examples and
many tickets use similar general support language.

The class-weighted Logistic Regression model improved macro F1 for the
priority classes, but overall priority prediction remains challenging.
